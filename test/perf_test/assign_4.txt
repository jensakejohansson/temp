Q: Assuming 50 simultaneous users:
1. What is the 95th percentile of response time? 
	All operations average at around 46s.
2. What is the throughput?
	The throughput reaches approx. 16 rps.


Q: How many simultaneous users can the application handle
& reliably provide responses?

A: The application can handle around 350 users before it starts delivering
unreliable responses. (see stats.png in this dir)


Q: Max throughput with reliable responses?

A: The application can reach approximately 102rps before responses become
unreliable.


Q: Under which circumstances do add calculations take longer than usual?

A: Since the issue is not connected to the number of simultaneous users, 
we have to assume that the issue is triggered by the user input. Due to the 
application's limited resources, equations with exceedingly large numbers or
bad notation (e.g. 5++5 or 5+-5) can cause the spike in response time.


Q: App crashes from time to time, why?

A: The application crashes once the working memory load starts exceeding 1.5GB.
Due to the restriction in docker-compose-perf.yml, the application starts
increasing stress on other PC components like CPU and disk writing, which
leads to higher response times and the eventual application crash.
